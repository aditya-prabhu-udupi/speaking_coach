Help

Tips for a good recording
  • Sit in a quiet room, about a hand's length from the mic.
  • Speak naturally. Don't try to hide your usual habits.
  • Talk for 30 to 60 seconds on any topic.
  • Use headphones with a mic if your room echoes.
How your score works

You start at 100. Points come off for a pace outside 120–160 WPM, filler words like "um" and "like", long pauses, repeated words and grammar slips. Use it to track progress, not as a grade.

Your privacy

Audio is sent to the server only to build your report, then deleted. Past sessions stay in this browser, and only you can see them.
